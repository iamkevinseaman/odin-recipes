Grilled Cheese

Description

Grilled cheese is a beloved, simple sandwich that transforms a few humble ingredients into something deeply satisfying. At its core, it consists of slices of bread toasted to golden perfection with butter and filled with melted cheese that stretches with each bite. The contrast between the crispy, buttery exterior and the gooey, molten center makes it a nostalgic favorite for kids and adults alike.

While the classic version uses white bread and American or cheddar cheese, grilled cheese is endlessly customizable. You can elevate it with sourdough or rye, mix cheeses like gouda, fontina, or gruyère, or add extras like tomatoes, bacon, or caramelized onions for new layers of flavor. Whether paired with a bowl of tomato soup or enjoyed on its own, grilled cheese is a timeless comfort food that’s quick to make and always hits the spot.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread
  • 1 Tablespoon mayonaise
  • 2 slices American cheese

Steps

      Preheat frying pan or saucepan over medium heat.
      Spread mayonaise on one side of each slice of bread.
      Place one slice of bread mayonaise-side down on the heated pan, add both slices of cheese, and top with last slice of bread mayonaise-side up.
      Cook until bottom side is golden brown, then flip the sandwich and brown the other side while melting the cheese inside.
