Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian comfort dish made by layering sheets of pasta with rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel or ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella, then baking until golden and bubbling. Each layer melds into the next, creating a hearty, satisfying casserole with both bold and comforting flavors. While traditional lasagna often features a meat-based ragù, variations include vegetables, spinach, or even seafood for different tastes and dietary needs.

The magic of lasagna lies in its balance of textures and flavors: tender pasta, savory sauce, creamy cheese, and a golden, slightly crispy top. It’s an ideal make-ahead dish, as the flavors deepen over time, and it freezes well for future meals. Whether served at a casual family dinner or a festive gathering, lasagna is a crowd-pleaser that brings warmth and nostalgia to the table.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound sweet italian sausage
  • 3/4 pound lean gorund beef
  • 1/2 cup minced onion
  • 2 cloves garlen, crushed
  • 1 (28 ouce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 (6.5 ounce) cans tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 Tablespoons white sugar
  • 4 Tablespoons chopped fresh parsley, divided
  • 1 1/2 teasoons dried basil leaves
  • 1 1/2 teasponns salt, divided, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 16 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 pound mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Gather all your ingredients
  2. Cook sausage, ground beef, onion and garlic over medium heat until weel browned.
  3. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, and water. Season with sugar, parsley, basil, salt, italian seasoning, fennel seeds, and pepper. Simmer, covered for 1 1/2 hours sturring occasionally.
  4. Cook lasagna noodles
  5. Combine ricotta with egg.
  6. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
  7. Layer ingredients, cover with foil and bake for25 minutes covered, then uncover and bake for 25 more.
